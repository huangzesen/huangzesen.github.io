I am a heliophysicist at UCLA. I study the Alfvénic structures that fill the solar wind, from the magnetic switchbacks Parker Solar Probe flies through near the Sun to the Alfvénon: a three-dimensional solitary Alfvén wave, which I construct as a nonlinear solution of ideal MHD and simulate on GPUs.

I also build infrastructure for AI-driven science. LingTai is an open-source runtime for lifelong research agents. ScienceIDE turns real scientific codebases into environments where agents are evaluated and trained. Since October 2026 I have led the astronomy track of the Agent Last Exam, which maps the open problems of astronomy into tasks for AI agents.

I received my Ph.D. in Geophysics and Space Physics from UCLA in 2024, and my dissertation was awarded the 2024 IAU PhD Prize of Division E (Sun and Heliosphere).
