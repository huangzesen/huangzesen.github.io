The trips

Newest first. Days count the days with a located photo.

The roads are not recorded tracks. Each line is either a plan I saved before the trip or the usual driving route between the places where photos were taken, so it shows where a trip went, not every turn. Miles are measured along those lines.

Stops are groups of photos about a kilometre across, named by town or sight. Nothing within 35 km of central Los Angeles is drawn: every road is cut at that circle and joined to the city centre by a straight line. Photo captions, exact photo positions and lodging are left out.

Day trips are included when the drive is at least 100 miles. One trip to Egypt is here too; its line is the saved plan, not a drive. Dates are the camera's, without time-zone correction.

Routes between photo stops come from OSRM on © OpenStreetMap contributors' data. Land and borders are from Natural Earth and geoBoundaries.
