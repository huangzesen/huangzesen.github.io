Explore an Alfvénon in 3D interactive

The Alfvénon from Huang et al. (2026a), θmax ≈ 45°, on its 128³ grid. Nested surfaces show θ = cos⁻¹(Bx/|B|) from 0° to 45°. The field lines (grey) are traced from the most-deflected cell and its neighbours; the red one starts at that cell. Drag to rotate, scroll or pinch to zoom, and shift-drag to pan. Built with PyVista and vtk.js.
