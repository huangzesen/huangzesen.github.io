# Agentic AI Summit 2026 — evidence analysis

This public-safe bundle presents a source-labeled analysis of the Berkeley Agentic AI Summit held on August 1–2, 2026. Facts are separated from interpretations, auto-caption excerpts are labeled, and missing Aug-2 transcript coverage remains an explicit gap.

## Evidence snapshot

- 43 published schedule blocks
- 178 indexed people: 169 program-mapped speakers plus 9 featured-only people not joined to a published program block
- 202 session-speaker join rows
- 157 organizations
- 11 ended official recordings validated in the accepted local manifest
- 8 normalized Aug-1 English auto-caption files / 68,496 cues
- 13 audited claims: 11 facts and 2 interpretations

## Files

- `index.html` — standalone interactive analysis page
- `analysis.json` — structured claims, explorer data, coverage, leads, and explicit gaps
- `sources.json` — public-safe source registry and claim-to-source map
- `build_candidate.py` — standard-library build script; requires the accepted local evidence corpus named in the script
- `qa_check.py` / `qa_report.json` — deterministic static validation and receipt

## Limits

Schedule evidence is not a transcript. Auto captions are not human-edited. A stage recording that spans multiple schedule blocks does not justify guessed speaker attribution. Company and role notes do not establish endorsement, funding, customer use, a relationship, or permission to contact/apply. Six Aug-2 transcript entries were unavailable in the accepted corpus at the analysis cutoff.
